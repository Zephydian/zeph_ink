# Welcome to Zeph#Ink

Zeph#Ink is a calm place to write. Everything here is saved as **plain markdown**, so your writing opens anywhere, forever.

## Try it out

- Pick a style from the **Body** menu in the toolbar
- Start a new line, type `##` and a space to make a heading
